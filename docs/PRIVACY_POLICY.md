# Privacy Policy — Sasi (Pocket Companion)

**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

Sasi is a pocket-companion app by Sree. This policy explains what the app
does with your information. Short version: **everything stays on your device.**

## Data the app uses

- **Display over other apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`).** Used only to draw the
  Sasi character on your screen. The app cannot see or record what is
  underneath the overlay.
- **Usage stats (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`).** Used only on your device to
  calculate screen-time totals and session lengths so Sasi can remind you to
  rest. These numbers never leave your phone.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`).** Used only to show reminder
  notifications from Sasi.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising,
no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network
requests at all.

## Data storage

Your settings (companion name, reminder times, theme) are stored locally on
your device using Android's DataStore. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Children

Sasi is family-friendly and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship with the app.
